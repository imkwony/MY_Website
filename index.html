01About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, advised by Professor Hyeoncheol Kim at the Intelligence and Informatics (INI) Lab.

My research applies machine learning and data science to education, with a particular emphasis on causal inference for addressing educational problems.

Before my doctoral studies, I served as a secondary school teacher under the Seoul Metropolitan Office of Education for four and a half years, teaching Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence.

I am interested in data-driven research in education and welcome opportunities for academic discussion and research collaboration.
